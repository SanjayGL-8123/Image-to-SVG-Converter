## 1. Overview & Core Privacy Guarantee

At **VectorCraft AI**, we believe your personal data, images, and proprietary graphics belong strictly to you. This Privacy Policy outlines how your data is handled when using both the **VectorCraft AI Web Application** and the **Desktop Python Application**.

> 🔒 **100% Local On-Device Processing Guarantee**  
> All image processing, color quantization, contour tracing, and SVG vector generation take place **entirely on your local machine** within your web browser or local Python execution environment. **No images or converted files are ever uploaded, transmitted, saved to remote cloud servers, or processed externally.**

---

## 2. Data Collection & Processing

### A. Uploaded Images & Graphics
- **Local Memory Only:** Any JPEG, PNG, WEBP, or GIF image you upload, drag & drop, or paste from clipboard is processed in temporary local memory (RAM / HTML5 Canvas).
- **No Remote Transmission:** Your original raster images and generated SVG files **never leave your device**.
- **Session Cleanup:** Closing the browser tab or exiting the desktop application immediately purges all transient image data from memory.

### B. Personal Data & Telemetry
- **Zero Telemetry:** We do **not** collect personal identification information, names, email addresses, IP logs, or device identifiers.
- **Zero Analytics:** VectorCraft AI does not use tracking pixels, Google Analytics, telemetry scripts, or user behavior trackers.
- **No Cookies:** The application does not read, write, or store tracking cookies. Local storage is limited solely to preserving your UI theme preference (e.g. Dark/Light mode toggle).

---

## 3. Third-Party Dependencies

- **Web Fonts:** The web application loads standard open-source typography (*Plus Jakarta Sans* & *Fira Code*) via Google Fonts. Google's privacy policy applies to standard web font requests.
- **No Third-Party Advertising:** We do not partner with ad networks or display third-party advertisements.

## 4. User Rights & Ownership

- **100% Asset Ownership:** You retain 100% full ownership, copyright, and intellectual property rights over all SVG vector files created or converted using VectorCraft AI.
- **Commercial Use:** You are free to use your converted SVG vector graphics for personal, commercial, and open-source projects without restriction or attribution requirements.

## 5. Security Statement

Because all computation is executed locally on your device:
- Your files are immune to cloud data breaches, man-in-the-middle network interception, or server-side leaks.
- You maintain complete air-gapped security when processing sensitive logos, trademarks, or confidential design documents.
